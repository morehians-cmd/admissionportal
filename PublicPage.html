Who may apply

Admission Overview

Moreh Academy's Basic Education grade configuration covers Nursery, Kinder 1, Kinder 2, and Grades 1–12. Grade placement and availability are subject to the school's current admission guidelines.

Step 1

Inquire with the Admissions Office

Contact or visit the Admissions Office to ask about the current school year, available grade levels, application schedule, and instructions.

Step 2

Obtain Application Instructions

Get the current application instructions and forms from the school. Provide the learner, parent or guardian, and contact information requested by the Admissions Office.

Step 3

Prepare and Submit Requirements

Ask the Admissions Office for the current checklist for the applicant's grade level. School requirements are submitted personally to the school. Admission personnel review the documents and update the applicant's profile.

Step 4

Assessment or Interview

Coordinate any assessment, interview, or additional evaluation required by the school for the applicant's grade level.

Step 5

Admission Decision

Receive the admission decision and follow the enrollment instructions provided by the school.

Step 6

Complete Enrollment

Complete the remaining enrollment steps through the school's official process and coordinate with the appropriate office.

Academics

From the early years through Senior High School, Moreh Academy's academic pathway supports learners as they build foundational knowledge, discover their strengths, and prepare for their next stage.

Early Childhood

Nursery, Kinder 1 and Kinder 2

Early childhood education provides a foundation for language, numeracy, social development, and school readiness through guided learning experiences.

Elementary

Grades 1–6

Elementary education develops essential knowledge, learning habits, character, and foundational competencies across key learning areas.

Junior High School

Grades 7–10

Junior High School strengthens core academic skills and gives learners opportunities to explore interests and prepare for Senior High School pathways.

Senior High School

Grades 11–12

Senior High School prepares learners for higher education, employment, and entrepreneurship through specialized learning pathways.

Senior High School Clusters

The following cluster descriptions reflect program references in the repository. Confirm current offerings, subject lists, and specializations with the Academic Office.

ASSH

Arts, Social Sciences, and Humanities

Explores culture, human behavior, media, law, and communication. Related career fields may include education, journalism, psychology, and law.

BE

Business and Entrepreneurship

Introduces enterprise, financial planning, and marketing, supporting learners interested in business, accounting, and entrepreneurship.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

Emphasizes scientific discovery, healthcare, engineering, and research, preparing learners for further study in related fields.

ICT

ICT Support and Computer Programming Technologies

Covers areas such as software, web development, networks, and hardware, building foundations for technology-related study and careers.

HT

Hospitality and Tourism

Introduces guest experience, culinary arts, and event planning for learners interested in hospitality and tourism-related fields.
