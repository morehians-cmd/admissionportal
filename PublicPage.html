Who we are

About Moreh Academy

Moreh Academy is a learning community in Taguig City committed to developing knowledge, character, faith and purpose. Our school serves learners from early education through Grade 12.

Our purpose

Vision

To nurture learners who pursue righteousness, excellence and meaningful contribution to their communities.

Our commitment

Mission

To provide values-driven education that develops the whole learner through quality teaching and supportive relationships.

Our community

Core Values

Learning, integrity, responsibility, compassion and excellence guide the Moreh Academy community.
