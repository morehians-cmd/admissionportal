How to enroll

Six steps from account to classroom.

Create an account

Register with your LRN and email to open your portal account.

Complete your student profile

Fill out all required personal, family and emergency contact details in the portal.

Print the Student Enrollment Form

Print the form and have it signed by you and your parent or legal guardian.

Print the Parental Consent & Data Privacy Undertaking Form

This form is filled out and signed only by your parent or legal guardian.

Submit your requirements

Bring all required physical documents to the Admissions Office in person.

Track your status

Sign in any time to see your requirements and enrollment status.

Your application, in one place.

Start your application, or sign in to check requirements and follow your status.
